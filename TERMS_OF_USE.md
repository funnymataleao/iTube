The welcome period does not create a StoreKit transaction, schedule a charge, or require cancellation. When the period ends, you can dismiss the offer and continue using the free version.

iTube offers monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions and a non-consumable lifetime purchase. These purchases are available from Settings during or after the welcome period. A paid subscription starts immediately when you confirm it through Apple's purchase interface; the local welcome period is not a subscription trial and does not delay billing. The exact localized price and billing period shown by Apple's purchase interface at confirmation control what you pay.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Apple charges and manages the purchase through your Apple Account. On Apple TV, manage or cancel a subscription in **Settings → Profiles and Accounts → your profile → Subscriptions**. You can restore eligible purchases inside iTube.
