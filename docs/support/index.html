iTube Support

Quick answers for Apple TV, purchases, sign-in, and playback.

Watching without an account

Google sign-in is optional. You can browse and play available videos as a guest. If the Plus screen appears, choose Continue Free to return to the app.

Plans unavailable

A StoreKit connection problem must not block free use. Continue free, check that Apple TV is online, and try loading plans again later from Settings.

A video will not play

Confirm that Apple TV can reach the internet, try another video, and install the latest iTube version. Some videos can be unavailable because of region, age, account, or third-party service restrictions.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases in iTube Settings. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple TV Settings → Profiles and Accounts → your profile → Subscriptions.

Contact the developer

For help that is not covered above, email mail@denisefremov.com.

Include the Apple TV model, tvOS version, iTube version, the action you took, and the exact message shown on screen. A video URL or ID is useful for playback reports.

Protect your account. Never send passwords, OAuth codes, cookies, payment-card details, or other secrets. Apple handles App Store billing and refund requests.

Source and acknowledgements

The source repository and open-source acknowledgements are available on GitHub. For private account or privacy matters, use email rather than a public issue.
