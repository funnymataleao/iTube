The experience

Open it and start watching.

iTube does not require an account or purchase to browse and play available videos. Sign-in is there only when you want account features.

Guest access

No registration gate

Browse, search, follow channels locally, and watch without connecting a Google account.

Native playback

Built around AVKit

Use familiar Apple TV controls for playback, captions, audio tracks, chapters, and the queue.

Standalone

No companion Mac

Playback runs on Apple TV itself. An always-on computer or local relay is not required.

iTube Plus

Optional support, clearly presented.

Plans are available from Settings at any time. After the first seven days, iTube may show a dismissible Plus offer. Dismissing it keeps the free experience available.

No automatic charge is created by the welcome period. Purchases start only after confirmation through Apple's StoreKit interface. The App Store purchase sheet shows the applicable localized price, duration, renewal terms, and eligibility.

Independent software

Transparent about the services it uses.

iTube is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or YouTube. Online catalog, account, and playback features depend on third-party services.
