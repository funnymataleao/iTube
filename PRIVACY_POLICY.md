# iTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

iTube is a third-party Apple-platform client for browsing and playing online video. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices available to you. iTube is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or YouTube.

## Summary

- The iTube tvOS app does not use advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated analytics service.
- Network requests are required to search for, browse, and play online content. The services receiving those requests can receive your IP address and standard network metadata.
- Google sign-in is optional. If you sign in, iTube accesses the YouTube information and actions needed for the features you choose to use.
- Apple processes in-app purchases. iTube receives signed transaction and entitlement information, but never receives your full payment-card details.

## Information handled by iTube

### Google and YouTube account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, iTube may access your account display name, profile image, YouTube channel identity, subscriptions, playlists, liked videos, and watch-history-related data. It may also send actions that you explicitly request, such as subscribing to a channel, changing a playlist, rating a video, or recording watch progress.

OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored in the Apple Keychain on your device. Account display information, cookies required for the signed-in session, and selected account/channel identifiers may also be stored locally so that the app can remain signed in. iTube uses this information only to provide the account features visible in the app. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train a general-purpose AI model.

iTube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

Your use of YouTube through iTube is also subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Playback, browsing, and third-party services

To provide its core features, iTube sends requests to Google/YouTube services and content delivery networks. These requests can include video or channel identifiers, search terms you submit, locale and playback capability information, authentication credentials when signed in, and normal network metadata.

When enabled, community features such as SponsorBlock and DeArrow send a video identifier to their respective public services to retrieve segment or metadata suggestions. Those services can receive normal network metadata. If you configure a custom token or relay service, requests sent to that service are governed by the operator's policy.

### Purchases and the welcome period

Apple processes purchases and subscriptions. iTube reads StoreKit product information and cryptographically signed transaction data, including product identifier, entitlement status, expiration or revocation status, and whether an eligible purchase is Family Shared. Apple handles billing credentials and payment processing.

iTube stores the date of first use and the date when the optional Plus offer was last presented in local app preferences. These dates schedule the non-blocking seven-day welcome period and a limited reminder cadence. They are not sent to the developer. Removing and reinstalling the app can reset this local period.

### Information stored on your device

iTube can store app settings, local subscriptions, search and viewing history, playback position, cached images and metadata, and queue state on your device.

The tvOS app does not send developer analytics or advertising telemetry. Other platform builds may offer an explicit diagnostic-report action; a report is sent only when you choose that action and can contain crash and technical diagnostic information.

## Retention and deletion

Local settings and caches remain until you reset them or remove the app. Selecting **Sign Out** removes iTube's locally stored Google session credentials and account state. Selecting **Disconnect & Delete Local Data** also removes local follows, search and watch history, playback positions, feeds, queue, and cached account data. You can manage or delete information held by Google through your Google and YouTube account controls, and revoke the app's access through your Google Account security settings.

Purchase records are retained by Apple according to Apple's policies and cannot be deleted by iTube. Removing the app does not cancel an active subscription.

## Sharing

iTube does not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed to provide a feature you request, to protect security, to comply with law, or as otherwise described in this policy. The principal recipients are Apple for purchases and platform services; Google/YouTube for account, catalog, and playback functionality; content delivery networks; and optional community or user-configured services described above.

## Children

iTube is not directed to children and does not knowingly create profiles of children. Google/YouTube and Apple account eligibility and parental-control rules continue to apply. Families should use Apple TV purchase restrictions and the content controls available in their accounts.

## Security

iTube uses platform security features such as the Apple Keychain and StoreKit transaction verification. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and availability also depends on the security practices of the external services you choose to use.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its date. When a change requires new consent, iTube will request it before using account data for the new purpose.
